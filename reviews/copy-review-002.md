# Copy review 002 — the home page, first screen

**Written:** 2026-09-22
**Why:** Phase 0 of the 2026-09-22 site audit. The home page's first screen
undersells the work and still points at four roles when plan-001 §6 Q1 chose one.
**Scope:** `public/index.html` only — title, meta, hero, and the "What I am looking
for" band. Nothing here is live; the current wording stays until you rule.
Facts used below all come from the live site, plan-001 or copy-review-001. Nothing new
is asserted — where a proposal would need a new fact, it is a question instead.

---

## How to answer this without typing much

Same as copy-review-001. Reply with the block number and a mark:

- `OK` — take the proposal as written
- `KEEP` — leave the current wording
- `A` / `B` — pick an option
- `FIX` — right idea, wrong words; say what's off
- `CUT` — delete the block

e.g. *"R1 A, R2 B, R3 OK, R4 FIX — say 'the society' not 'a nonprofit', R5 OK, Q1 yes"*

### R1 — page title (browser tab, search result, link preview)

> **Now:** Thomas Cheesman - AI-assisted web builder

**⚠ FLAG.** "AI-assisted" is what everyone claims in 2026; it names the tool, not the
job. Plan-001 chose **nonprofit technology** as the lead role.

> **A:** Thomas Cheesman - websites and digital operations for nonprofits
> **B:** Thomas Cheesman - nonprofit web and digital operations, built with AI

B keeps AI in the tab because it is part of the method. A lets the case studies show it.

### R2 — the headline (the H1)

> **Now:** Thomas Cheesman

**⚠ FLAG.** The biggest words on the page repeat the wordmark directly above them.
Proposal: your name moves into the small label line, and the H1 becomes the one
sentence that gets you hired.

> **Label:** Thomas Cheesman · Grande Prairie, Alberta · remote
> **A:** I run a nonprofit's website, and I hold it to a written standard.
> **B:** Websites and digital operations for small nonprofits.

A is first person and specific; B is a plain description of the job. The H1 stays
short either way: it is set at display size.

### R3 — the lede, first sentence

> **Now:** I run the website for a nonprofit and three more for practice and, eventually, work.

**⚠ FLAG — undersells.** "For practice" tells a hiring manager three of the four sites
don't count. They are live, public and maintained.

> **Proposed:** I have run the website for a volunteer nonprofit housing society since
> 2023, and built three more sites from scratch, each a different design.

**Question Q1:** is "since 2023" right for the GPRS *site* itself (copy-review-001
dates the board seat to June 2023, and the home page says "Live since 2023")?

### R4 — the lede, the rest

> **Now:** I set the objectives, design the thing, and then build it with AI. After
> seventeen years managing cooks, a team of AI is the easy part; knowing what to tell
> it is not.

Keep both sentences; this is your voice and the second line is the best one on the
page. One small risk: "the easy part" can read as flippant to someone wary of AI work.

> **A:** Keep as written.
> **B:** …After seventeen years managing cooks, directing a team of AI is familiar
> work; knowing what to tell it is the hard part.

### R5 — the body paragraph under the lede

> **Now:** Seventeen years running kitchens, then a career change. I have shipped four
> live sites — four deliberately different designs, not one template reused, this one
> included — and a research application that maps nearly four thousand official
> statistical reports and the documented dependencies between them. I am looking for
> remote work in web development, digital operations, accessibility, or nonprofit
> technology.

**⚠ FLAG.** "Seventeen years" appears in R4 and again here, one line apart. The
four-sites/different-designs point moved into R3. And four roles in one sentence reads
as "anything"; plan-001 names the other three once on Background and Contact instead.

> **Proposed:** I also built a research application that maps nearly four thousand
> official statistical reports and the documented dependencies between them. I am
> looking for remote work running a nonprofit's website and digital operations.

### R6 — "What I am looking for" band

> **Now (first sentence):** Remote work in web development, digital operations,
> accessibility, or nonprofit technology.

Same four-roles issue. The rest of the band is good and stays.

> **Proposed:** Remote work running the website and digital operations for a
> nonprofit or a small organisation. I am also open to web development and
> accessibility roles.

This keeps the other roles on the home page once, as secondary. Say `CUT` on the
second sentence if you want them on Background and Contact only.

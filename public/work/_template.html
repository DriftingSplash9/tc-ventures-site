The ask

What I wanted, in my own words

“Being a bit of a portfolio it should be unique, detailed, complex, and push the limits of what is modern with AI-assisted web design. It needs a ‘wow’ factor. Also, the contact page is not done and will get some special attention because contact pages always seem like a lame afterthought and they are generic.”

brief-001 · 2026-09-11 · the brief

I was looking for work, and “I build with AI” proves nothing on its own. So the site had to show it: built to the same rules it describes, with the working files left public for anyone to check.

The standard

What “good” meant, written down first

Every session started from a handoff file that carried the rules, and every session ended by writing the next one. The rules are short because most of them were born from a specific mistake, and a rule that is longer than the mistake it prevents does not get read.handoff-013 §3

The loop each session ran through

The brief is mine. The spec says what good means before anything is built. The agent builds. I review every block and rule on it. Whatever I corrected becomes a rule. The rule goes into the next handoff, and the next session starts from there.

What the AI got wrong

Three misses, all real, all caught before they shipped

The agents that built this site were good. They were also confidently wrong in ways that would have cost me the reader’s trust, and none of the three would have shown up as an error anywhere.

  1. Names showed through a screensaver

    The Desk figure needed the monitor’s contents list hidden, because that list names people who do not belong on a hiring site. The agent drew a screensaver over it and reported the names hidden. At about two per cent brightness, they were still there, and readable when zoomed.

    Kind of miss: looked right, was not measured · handoff-013 §2, traps

  2. Exact counts in the copy

    The first drafts stated the graph’s report and dependency counts to the unit. The corpus grows every week, so the numbers would have been wrong within days of going live and the site would have been quietly lying from then on.

    Kind of miss: a true fact with an expiry date · copy-review-001

  3. “Available now,” five times

    A claim that rots. It reads well the day it is written and reads badly the day someone finds the page after I have started somewhere.

    Kind of miss: over-selling · copy-review-001, Q1
How I caught it

A measurement, a review, and a rule each time

  1. Stretched the contrast on the served file

    Not the file the agent wrote — the one the site actually served, pulled back down and pushed through a six-times contrast stretch. The names came up. The figure was re-cropped below the monitor, re-served, and checked the same way again.

    Became the rule: stretch contrast on any screen-bearing screenshot before it ships · handoff-013 §2

  2. Read every block, ruled on every block

    The whole site went into one numbered review — every paragraph a block, every block marked OK, CUT, FIX or SOFTEN. The counts became “nearly four thousand” and the exact figures were kept for one application screenshot, where they are true for the day it was taken.

    Became the rule: never publish an exact count in copy · copy-review-001

  3. Same review, question one

    The review flagged five instances of “available now” and asked: keep, soften or cut? All five became “open to work”.

    Became the rule: hiring voice — first person, past tense, verifiable, no selling · copy-review-001, Q1

What shipped

Five pages, one stylesheet, and the working files in the open

The site is live at tc-ventures.ca. Everything on it renders with JavaScript off; the one heavy thing, the live graph, waits for a click. The fonts are served from the same place as the page, so nothing outside the repo can rot underneath it.

The honest limits

As of September 2026 this is a skeleton: the pages say what I built and assert how I work, and this rebuild is what makes the second part visible. The accessibility pass is mine to do once the design and the copy are final, not before.

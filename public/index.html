Four things I have built

  1. The Economic Report Influence Graph

    A browser-based 3D map of where official numbers come from: which published report is calculated from which other report, across more than a hundred countries. Every line has to be backed by a quotable sentence in a real document, or it does not get drawn.

    Scale
    Nearly 4,000 reports and the dependencies between them, and still growing
    Built with
    TypeScript, React, Three.js

  2. The Back Quarter

    The homepage of my personal site is a quarter section of farmland you drive around in a buggy, where every building is a part of the site. It is the country I grew up in, rebuilt as the way in — and it degrades to an ordinary menu if the graphics never load.

    Built with
    Three.js · Matter · no build step
    Status
    Live — it is the front page of thomascheesman.ca

  3. The Desk and the Drawer

    That same site has a second menu that is a photograph of my desk — the monitor is the contents, the keyboard is search, a toad opens an arcade. The plain list is the default, and the desk and its games load only when a visitor asks for them.

    Built with
    Plain JavaScript and CSS · no framework, no build step
    Status
    Live, and the accessible path is the point

  4. Four live sites

    Written and maintained solo, and deliberately four different designs rather than one template reused: bareyourrare.org for rare-disease patients (read the case study), gpresidentialsociety.com for a volunteer-run nonprofit providing barrier-free housing (read the case study), thomascheesman.ca, and this one (read the case study). Accessibility and plain language are requirements on all four, not polish applied at the end.

    Built with
    PHP, CSS, JavaScript, custom WordPress themes; this one hand-written, no framework
    Live since
    2023

How I work

One

Specify before building

The specification is the work. I decide what “good” means, write the standard down where the next person can find it, and hold every change to it. My repositories carry protocols and playbooks because a standard that only lives in someone’s head drifts within a week.

Two

Evidence over assertion

On the influence graph the rule is no document, no edge: a dependency exists only if a published source says so in words I can quote. Leads I cannot source go to a dropped list with a reason. Every drawn line carries a grade for how well its citation actually supports it, and the grade is visible in the app.

Three

Verify by measuring

I check work against a clean rig rather than trusting that it looks right. A layout force I was about to ship turned out to have been calibrated against a measurement script with a bug in it. I caught that before it went out, threw out the calibration, and rebuilt it.

What I am looking for

Remote work running the website and digital operations for a nonprofit or a small organisation. I am also open to web development and accessibility roles. I am most useful where a small product needs someone to own it end to end — deciding what it should be, building it, and being accountable for whether it actually works.

I am comfortable being the only person on a thing, and comfortable being the one who says a result is not good enough yet. Open to work. More about the route I took here.
